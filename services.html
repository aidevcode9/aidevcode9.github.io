Services

Consulting offers designed around real decision points.

These engagements are built for moments when technical ambiguity is blocking a business decision: before a vendor commitment, before a production push, during diligence, or when a leadership team needs sharp outside guidance.

Common triggers

  • leadership needs an objective read before approving spend
  • an internal team is too close to the architecture to assess risk cleanly

AI Readiness Assessment

Get a risk-ranked view of what your organization can actually support.

This engagement is for teams that know AI matters but need a grounded assessment of readiness before they commit to a roadmap, a vendor, or a broader rollout.

  • review governance, data realities, architecture, and operating model
  • assess evaluation approach, observability, and release discipline
  • translate technical gaps into executive-level risk and sequencing
  • deliver a scorecard, briefing, and remediation roadmap

Typical fit

Before a major investment, after several fragmented AI experiments, or when leadership needs a structured go / no-go view.

Typical timeline: 2-3 weeks

Starting investment: $15K-$25K

AI Due Diligence Support

Pressure-test AI-heavy businesses, products, or vendors before the commitment becomes expensive.

Designed for leadership teams, advisors, or operators evaluating AI claims. The focus is not generic market commentary. It is whether the product, architecture, and operating model can support the claims being made.

  • evaluate architecture maturity, control stack, and delivery risk
  • review retrieval and model patterns, governance, and observability
  • highlight issues that could slow integration or break product claims
  • produce severity-ranked findings and a practical 100-day view

Typical fit

Vendor shortlists, diligence-style reviews, product assessments, or operating-team evaluations where AI is central to the value story.

Typical timeline: scoped per diligence window

Starting investment: $15K-$30K

Production AI Architecture Review

Review a live or near-live AI system before technical debt turns into operating debt.

Best for teams that already have code, a pilot, or a production launch underway. The review focuses on whether the system is durable, observable, and governable under real operating conditions.

  • assess grounding quality, evals, safeguards, and prompt defense posture
  • review tool use, orchestration, fallback logic, and release controls
  • inspect observability and failure-handling discipline
  • deliver prioritized findings with a stabilization plan

Typical fit

For product or platform teams that have momentum but need an independent read before scaling usage or visibility.

Typical timeline: 1-2 weeks

Starting investment: scoped per review

FAQ

How the engagements are usually structured.

The work is intentionally shaped to be lightweight enough to move quickly and strong enough to hold up in leadership conversations.

Do these services include implementation?

Sometimes. The default shape is assessment, advisory, review, and leadership support. If implementation help is useful after the review, it can be scoped separately.

Can you work alongside an internal engineering or product team?

Yes. That is often the best arrangement. Internal teams provide context and operating reality, while I provide an outside technical view and help sharpen decision quality.

What is the output leadership receives?

Typically a concise executive briefing, a severity-ranked findings set, and a practical next-step plan. The exact package depends on the engagement type.

Do you also take hands-on build work, or only advisory engagements?

Yes. The default positioning is advisory, review, and leadership support, but I also take selective embedded AI engineering sprints where the need is a senior builder working directly on RAG, orchestration, voice, or evaluation infrastructure.
